What is Goldbach's Comet?

Goldbach's conjecture (1742) states that every even integer greater than 2 can be expressed as the sum of two primes.

The Goldbach function g(n) counts how many such representations exist for each even n.

When plotted, these values form a distinctive pattern resembling a comet—hence the name. The "tail" shows fascinating structure related to prime distribution.
