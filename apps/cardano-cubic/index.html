Cardano’s cubic identity as volume

Start from one cube of side u+v, then pull it apart into a blue cube u³, a pink cube v³, and three matching prisms of volume uv(u+v) each.

(u+v)³ = u³ + v³ + 3uv(u+v)

Self-checks:

    What the animation shows

    The full body is a cube with side length u+v, so its volume is (u+v)³.

    The dissection separates out one cube of volume u³, one cube of volume v³, and three congruent rectangular boxes with dimensions u × v × (u+v).

    Because there are exactly three such boxes, their combined contribution is 3uv(u+v).

    Geometric heartbeat
