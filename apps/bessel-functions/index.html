About Bessel Functions

Bessel functions are solutions to Bessel's differential equation:

x²y'' + xy' + (x² - n²)y = 0

Jₙ(x) - Bessel function of the first kind, regular at the origin
Yₙ(x) - Bessel function of the second kind, singular at x=0

These functions appear in problems with cylindrical symmetry: wave propagation, heat conduction, vibrating membranes, and electromagnetic theory.
